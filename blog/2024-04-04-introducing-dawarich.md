So, I love having my locations visualized. I love [Google Maps Timeline](/docs/comparisons/vs-google-timeline), I just think Google knows enough about me as it is.

So I built Dawarich to claim control over my location tracking and, since I have all the data, I can calculate whatever statistics I want.

It's open-source and self-hostable, so you can do too.

<!-- truncate -->

If you've tracked your locations using Google Maps and/or [OwnTracks](/docs/comparisons/vs-owntracks), you can export your data and [upload it to Dawarich](/import-export).

[https://github.com/Freika/dawarich](https://github.com/Freika/dawarich)
