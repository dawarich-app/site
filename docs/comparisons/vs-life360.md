# Dawarich vs Life360: a private family location alternative

Life360 is a family safety and location sharing app that shows members of a Circle on a map.

Life360 focuses on family safety, including crash detection and emergency help. Dawarich focuses on **location history**, **data ownership**, and **privacy**, and offers [family location sharing](/family/) on Cloud and self-hosted instances. Both let members stop sharing their position. Dawarich adds a separate choice for sharing history and a location request that the recipient can decline. This comparison helps you choose the features your household needs.

## How family sharing works

On Dawarich Cloud, the [Family plan](/family/) covers up to five separate accounts. The owner invites each person by email; the recipient accepts the invitation using that address. Each person can then install the [iOS](/docs/dawarich-for-ios) or [Android](/docs/dawarich-for-android) app and sign in to their own account. The apps show family members on the map.

Every member controls their own live sharing. The Cloud plan owner starts with sharing on when the family is created and can turn it off; invited members start with sharing off. Each person can set a 1-, 6-, 12-, or 24-hour timer and choose separately whether to share recent history. If someone is not sharing, another member can send a location request; the recipient may accept or decline it. See the [Family feature guide](/docs/features/family) for the exact steps. Self-hosted families use the same sharing controls without a subscription.

Life360 also lets members [pause sharing for a Circle](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053695148823-Share-My-Location) and offers [temporary Circles](https://www.life360.com/features/temporary-circles). Its stronger fit is safety: crash detection, SOS alerts and driving reports are features Dawarich does not provide. Check [Life360's current plans](https://www.life360.com/plans-pricing) for availability in your region.

## Privacy and data control

Life360 says it stopped selling raw location data. Its [current privacy policy](https://legal.corp.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/16038777217175-Life360-Privacy-Policy) says some partners may commercialize aggregated, de-identified data where permitted. Dawarich does not sell location data; self-hosters can keep their data on their own server. Sharing your position with family is always a choice in either product, but the controls and history options differ.

Life360's [history help page](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053602225559-Location-Drive-History) says driving history cannot be exported from inside its app; its [Privacy Center](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053465742743-Privacy-Center) offers an account-data request.

## When to Choose Dawarich

- You care about **privacy** and want to own your location data
- You want **unlimited location history** — not just 2 or 30 days
- You want to **import data** from Google Timeline, GPX files, or other sources
- You want **visualizations** like heatmaps, statistics, and trip timelines
- You want a **web interface** for exploring your data on a big screen
- You want [**family location sharing**](/family/) where each person decides what they share, for how long, and whether others can see their history

## When to Choose Life360

- You need **safety features** such as crash detection and SOS alerts
- You want **driving reports** for teen drivers
- You don't need location history beyond 30 days
